# U15 Commands: movement and jumps   (owns ui/src/lib/commands/defs/nav/**, defs/move/** and tests; no components)
One file per command. Movement acts on selection.svelte.ts (ordered visible ids published by the views; for the board, columns + ids).
- move: j/k (down/up, with counts), g g (top), G (bottom), h/l (previous/next pane or board column), enter (open focused issue in the panel), escape (close panel / clear selection / step back, in that order), / (focus search = open palette in issue-search mode), x (toggle select focused), shift+j / shift+k (extend selection), mod+a (select all in view), space (peek).
- jumps: g i inbox (route exists as a placeholder), g m my issues, g v views, g p projects, g t timeline, g c cycles, g s settings, g d design gallery (dev only: `when` import.meta.env.DEV).
- Each has label, icon, group ('Navigation', 'Selection'). The selection store may need small additions (e.g. column model): you may edit ui/src/lib/selection.svelte.ts additively with tests.
Tests (drive the key engine with real KeyboardEvents on a document with a fake ordered list): each key does its move; counts; gg/G; shift extends; escape order; nothing fires while an input is focused.
