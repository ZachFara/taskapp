1. `ui/src/styles/tokens.css`: colour roles (frame, sheet, bands 1-4, ink, muted, border, focus, signal, positive, warning,
   selection, overlay), the 12 palette colours for icons/labels (keys from crates/core/src/palette.rs; real hex per theme,
   contrast-checked on sheet and on bands), type scale, spacing scale, radius, shadow (floating things only), motion
   (durations 80/140/220ms, one easing), z-index, row heights (32 list, 28 compact), sidebar and panel widths.
   Light = `:root`, dark = `[data-theme="dark"]`, and `prefers-color-scheme` when no data-theme is set. Structure it so a
   future theme is only a new block of token values (themes are planned later): components may reference role tokens only.
2. Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque, self-hosted woff2 under `ui/src/styles/fonts/` (the app works offline; no Google Fonts
   link at runtime). OFL licence file beside it. Tabular figures on by default for numbers. No monospace.
3. `ui/src/styles/base.css`: reset, focus ring, selection, scrollbars, reduced-motion.
4. Primitives in `ui/src/ui/` (Svelte 5 runes, each with a small test): Button (primary, quiet, danger; optional kbd
   hint), IconButton, TextInput, TextArea, Select, Checkbox, Switch, Menu + MenuItem, Popover, Dialog, Tooltip
   (action name + shortcut, 500ms hover delay, also on focus), Kbd, Pill/Chip, Tabs (layout switcher), PageHeader
   (view name, icon, tabs slot, filter slot, actions slot), GroupHeader (the legend band: tone step per status
   category, name, count, collapse, add), EmptyState (contour-line art drawn in SVG + title + one action), Toast,
   Spinner/Skeleton, Avatarless. Use bits-ui (already installed) under Menu/Popover/Dialog/Select/Tooltip for a11y.
5. Icons: `Icon.svelte` with one consistent 16px set drawn as inline SVG (about 40 icons: nav, layouts, actions,
   entity defaults; ids match `icon:<id>` from the backend). `StatusGlyph` (cairn stones seen from the side:
   backlog, unstarted, started, completed, canceled) and `PriorityGlyph` (contour rings: none, low, medium, high,
   urgent). REDRAW the stone glyphs: in the spike they are lumpy and "todo" reads as a dash. They must be told apart at a
   glance at 16px in both themes; show them at 16 and 32 in the gallery. `EntityIcon` renders `icon:<id>` or
   `emoji:<x>` in a palette colour.
6. `/design` route (`DesignGallery.svelte`, add one line to routes.ts): every token swatch and every primitive in all
   states. This is the screen the owner reviews.
7. `scripts/check-tokens.sh` (shellcheck-clean, with a self-test in scripts/test/): fails on hex colours, rgb()/hsl(),
   px font-size, raw ms durations in `ui/src/**/*.svelte` and `*.css` outside `ui/src/styles/`. It must pass on your own
   files. Do NOT wire it into gate.sh or CI yet (old components would fail); say the exact lines to add later.
8. `docs/decisions/0010-design-system.md` (decision record: direction B chosen over A "Night trail" and C "Stone
   ledger", why, themes deferred) and `docs/design-system.md` (tokens, component rules, do/don't, how to add a theme later).
